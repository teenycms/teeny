# Code of conduct

Teeny is for everyone, from first-time website owners to experienced developers. We want this to be a friendly place to ask, help and build.

## Be kind

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with people who are new to GitHub or to code.
- Assume good intent. Most mistakes are just mistakes.
- Give feedback about the work, never the person. Explain why, and suggest a better way.
- Respect different views and experience. It's fine to disagree; it's not fine to be rude.
- Keep it on topic, and keep other people's private details private.

## Not okay

- Insults, harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Comments that put people down because of who they are: age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender, identity, nationality, race, religion, sexuality or anything else.
- Sexual language or images, and unwanted attention.
- Spam, trolling or deliberately derailing a conversation.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.

## What happens

The maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who don't follow this code, in any space run by Teeny: the repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and wiki. We'll usually give a friendly reminder first, unless the behaviour is serious.

## Reporting

If someone makes you feel unwelcome, click **⋯ → Report content** on their comment and choose to report it to the maintainers. Reports are private and taken seriously, and we won't share who reported.
