# Teeny CMS: descriptions

---

## One line

A small website builder that keeps everything in plain files, so there's no database to set up.

## Short

A small, secure website builder that keeps everything in plain files. There's no database to set up. Upload it to almost any web host, open your browser, and edit your pages by clicking on the text.

---

## Long

Teeny CMS is a small content management system for people who want a good-looking website without having to learn how websites work. It's made for landing pages, portfolios, simple documentation, photo galleries and link-in-bio pages.

There's no database. Your pages, settings and messages are saved as plain files on your own server, so a backup is just a copy of two folders. If your web host runs PHP, and almost all of them do, you can install Teeny in a couple of minutes. Upload the folder, open the installer, pick a starting layout and a look, and you're in.

You edit on the page itself. Click a heading and start typing. Add sections from the side panel, such as an image with text, a price list, a gallery, a contact form or a countdown. Each section can sit on the left, on the right or in the middle, and can be narrow or full width. There are three themes to start from, Minimal, Dark and Luxury. You can change their colours and fonts, or save your own version as a new theme and share it.

It also includes the things small sites usually need:

- a contact form with an inbox
- newsletter sign-ups you can download as a spreadsheet
- visitor numbers that don't use cookies
- password-protected pages
- a "coming soon" mode
- separate accounts for owners and editors

Security was part of the plan from day one:

- Passwords are stored as strong hashes, and sign-in can ask for a code from your phone.
- Every form is protected against forged requests.
- Uploaded files are checked one by one.
- Plugins can add new kinds of sections, but they can't run code on your server. Any interactive part of a plugin runs in a sealed box, away from the rest of the site.

Teeny CMS is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0. Use it for your own site, for client work, or as a starting point for something of your own.

## Short feature list

- No database. Everything is stored in plain files.
- Installs in a couple of minutes on ordinary shared hosting.
- Edit text by clicking on it.
- 31 kinds of sections, each with its own position and width.
- Three themes, your own colours and fonts, and a light/dark switch for visitors.
- Contact form, newsletter sign-ups and privacy-friendly visitor numbers.
- Owners and editors, two-factor sign-in, and backups in one click.
- Themes and plugins that are safe to install.
- Free and open source (Apache License 2.0).
