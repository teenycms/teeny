## Why Teeny

1. **Easy for everyone.** Click any text on your page and type. No code, no manuals. It installs in about two minutes, and you can try it in your browser first.
2. **No database, no migrations.** It runs on the cheapest shared hosting or a basic VPS. Updating means copying new files, with no upgrade scripts that can break your site.
3. **Your whole site is a folder.** Back it up by zipping it, move it by copying it, or keep it in a private Git repo. You also get a one-click backup in the Studio and the last 20 versions of every page.
4. **Fast by default.** Visitors get ready-made pages and about 12 KB of code. There are no heavy queries, no plugin bloat, and nothing to compile. The whole app is a 200 KB download.

## Long

Teeny CMS is a simple website builder for people who want a good-looking site without learning how websites work. It's made for landing pages, portfolios, simple documentation, photo galleries and link-in-bio pages.

**It's easy.** You edit on the page itself. Click a heading and start typing. Add sections from the side panel, such as an image with text, a price list, a gallery, a contact form or a countdown. Each section can sit on the left, on the right or in the middle, and can be narrow or full width. There are three themes to start from, Minimal, Dark and Luxury, with your own colours and fonts.

**There's no database.** If your web host runs PHP, and almost all of them do, you can install Teeny in a couple of minutes: upload the folder, open the installer, pick a layout and a look, and you're in. It runs happily on the cheapest shared hosting or a basic VPS. Updating is just copying new files over the old ones. There are no migrations and no upgrade scripts.

**Your whole site is a folder.** Pages, settings and messages are saved as plain files on your own server. A backup is a copy of two folders. Zip them, copy them to a new host, or keep them in a private Git repo. The Studio also has a one-click backup, and every page keeps its last 20 versions so you can go back.

**It's fast by default.** Visitors get pages that are already built, so most visits just read one file. A page needs only about 12 KB of styles and scripts. There are no database queries, plugins can't slow down your server, and the whole app is a 200 KB download with nothing to compile.

It also includes the things small sites usually need:

- a contact form with an inbox
- newsletter sign-ups you can download as a spreadsheet
- visitor numbers that don't use cookies
- password-protected pages
- a "coming soon" mode
- separate accounts for owners and editors

And it's safe by default: strong password hashing, optional two-factor sign-in, protection against forged requests, careful checks on every upload, and plugins that can't run code on your server.

Teeny CMS is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0. Use it for your own site, for client work, or as a starting point for something of your own.
